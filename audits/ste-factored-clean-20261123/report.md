## Per-recording factorization implemented, not automatic certification

tools/factored-native-clean.cjs consumes independent SHA-bound suffix and
prefix evidence. It does not contain song IDs, enumerate a catalog product,
install an index or claim a pair is playable. A proposal still needs original
pair rendering/exposure and both-platform actual-player validation.

Source Gilgamesh's published eight native suffix receipts and Sono Ok's
published prefix/exposure are imported without re-decoding or re-rendering
the positive. Per-channel quiet RMS is conservatively bounded by sqrt(2)
times the measured stereo RMS. This is an upper bound, not a new measurement.
Intervals must be fully covered; old eight-second negative cannot borrow a
quiet suffix measured later. The old energetic short tail stays rejected.

stereo from zero, maximum 3.5 seconds, no models or new searches. Djo fails

The existing iPhone path ALREADY has a confident-tail direct-handoff rule.
Repeating a normal silence skip would not be a new mix and is not counted.

## Documentation bounds

[WebKit's autoplay guidance](https://webkit.org/blog/7734/auto-play-policy-changes-for-macos/)
describes per-element grants and checking rejected play promises on macOS.
It does not prove iPhone behavior. The
[archived iOS considerations](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/Using_HTML5_Audio_Video/Device-SpecificConsiderations/Device-SpecificConsiderations.html)
are dated December 2012 and even discuss historical unsupported playbackRate.
Their single-stream statement must not be asserted as a verified rule for
current iOS. This is why measured hardware evidence is required rather than
either assuming two elements will work or declaring them impossible.
